## How to contribute

1. Open an issue first for anything larger than a bug fix, so the approach can
   be agreed on before you spend time on it.
2. Fork, branch, and keep each pull request focused on one change.
3. Match the surrounding code style. Add tests where the project has tests.
4. Submit only code you wrote yourself. If your patch includes third party
   code, or code generated from a source with its own license, say so
   explicitly in the pull request and name the license. See "If you do not own
   all of it" below.

## Signing the CLA

Every pull request must be covered by the agreement below. Pick one:

* **Bot (preferred).** A CLA bot, for example <https://cla-assistant.io>,
  comments on your first pull request with a link. You click to agree, and the
  signature is recorded against your account. Paste the "Individual
  Contributor License Agreement" section below into the gist the bot is
  configured with, so the bot and this file never drift apart.
* **Manually.** Post this as a comment on your pull request, from the account
  that authored the commits:

      I have read the CONTRIBUTING.md of this project and I agree to the
      Individual Contributor License Agreement it contains, for this and
      all my future contributions to this project.

A pull request cannot be merged before it is covered.

### If you contribute for your employer

If you wrote the contribution on work time, on employer equipment, or within
the scope of your employment, your employer may own the copyright, and you
cannot grant what you do not own. In that case either have an authorized
person at your employer agree to this document on the company's behalf, or
send an employer waiver to the maintainers before submitting. The Harmony
Entity CLA, linked at the end of this file, is the corporate form of this
agreement if your employer wants one.

### If you do not own all of it

If your contribution contains work you did not author, do not silently mix it
in. Open the pull request with a clear list of what came from elsewhere, where
it came from, and under what license, and leave its copyright headers intact.
The maintainers will decide whether it can be accepted. Vendored dependencies
are handled separately from this agreement.

### What this agreement covers

The agreement applies from your Effective Date, which is the earlier of the
date you agree to it and the date of your first contribution. It covers the
contributions you submit from then on. Contributions merged before you agreed
to it are not retroactively covered, so if you have contributed here before,
please post the manual statement above once and it will cover your past and
future work.
